Position size and exits

1R is the trade's original stop distance. Risk-based lots round down and require a price, volatility or swing stop. Equity/dollar stops use fixed lots. Price jumps can exceed planned loss.

Account limits and news

Drawdown is measured from the highest whole-account equity in this EA risk session. Reaching the limit closes EA positions and switches the EA off. The loss-streak limit switches it off without closing other trades. Manual positions are never closed by EA controls. Account zero-equity liquidation still applies to every position.
